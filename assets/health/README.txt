Health reminder character frames (played by HealthReminders.ps1).

Character: "AJ" from Adobe Mixamo (https://www.mixamo.com), used under the Adobe Mixamo terms.
Mixamo characters and animations are royalty-free for personal and commercial projects.
Clips (Mixamo names): Male Standard Walk (in place), Aj Waving Gesture, Male Drinking,
Stretching Arms By Pushing The Elbows.

Rendered with three.js in Chrome (transparent background), 20 fps, 180x210 px PNGs shown at 90x105 DIP.
Layout: <variant>\<clip>\NNN.png
  variant: water (holds a bottle in the left hand), stretch (no prop)
  clip:    walkL, walkR, hello, act (drinking or arm stretching)
